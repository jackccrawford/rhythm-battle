RHYTHM BATTLE

Evalyn's Game

Press Enter or tap Play

Choose your rival

How to play

  1. Watch your rival sing a pattern on the left.
  2. Copy it! The same notes fly into your lanes on the right.
  3. Hit each arrow when it touches the outline. Every note you hit plays the melody!
  4. Fill the bar to your side to win the battle.
